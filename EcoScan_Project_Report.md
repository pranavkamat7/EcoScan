## 📌 1. Project Introduction & Overview

### What is EcoScan?
**EcoScan** is a community-powered web application designed to encourage students, families, and local neighbours to take active care of their environment. 

In many neighbourhoods, we often see littered street corners, overflowing trash on footpaths, or discarded plastic bottles along riverbanks and playgrounds. People often walk past because they feel alone, or they think: *"Someone else will clean it up."*

EcoScan changes that mind-set by turning cleanup activities into an **inspiring, rewarding, and fun team activity**. 

With EcoScan, anyone can:
1. **Spot & Snap:** Take a picture of a littered area and pin it on a live neighbourhood map.
2. **Clean & Prove:** Clean the spot individually or with friends, take a verified "After" photo, and create a split **Before & After comparison slider**.
3. **Earn Civic Rewards:** Earn points, unlock achievement badges, rise on the community leaderboard, and receive certificates of honour.

> **Project Slogan:** *Clean your neighbourhood. Capture proof. Earn rewards.*

## 💡 2. Why We Built It Like This (Design Philosophy)

When building EcoScan for school and community use, we made conscious design choices to make it human, practical, and motivating:

### 1. Photo-First Proof ("Seeing is Believing")
Words alone cannot show the real effort volunteers put into cleaning an area. That is why **the interactive Before & After slider is the hero** of EcoScan. 
- You can drag the slider left and right to see the exact dirty spot transformed into a sparkling, clean space.
- It provides immediate emotional satisfaction: volunteers instantly feel proud of what they accomplished.

### 2. Gamification: Making Good Deeds Fun
Students love challenges, points, and friendly competition. We added a **Civic Points System**:
- Reporting a dirty spot earns **+20 Scout Points**.
- Cleaning a spot earns between **+100 to +300 Bounty Points** depending on the amount of waste cleared.
- Points help users climb the **Community Leaderboard** from *"Eco Scout"* to *"Civic Champion"*.

### 3. Neighbourhood Transparency with Live Maps
Instead of reports disappearing into a black box, every dirty spot and restored area is visible on an interactive map. 
- **Coral red pins** show areas that currently need cleaning.
- **Emerald green pins** celebrate areas that have been restored.
- Anyone can click a pin to see its location and directions.

### 4. Fresh, Natural, and Clean Visual Design
- **Navy & Obsidian:** Represents authority, clarity, and strong focus.
- **Vibrant Emerald Green:** Symbolizes nature, cleanliness, and active community positive energy.
- **Bright White & Slate:** Gives the app a clean, crisp notebook feel without clutter.

## 📱 4. How to Use the App (Step-by-Step Guide)

Using EcoScan is as simple as taking a photograph:

### Step 1: Open the Feed
When you open EcoScan, you immediately see the **Community Feed**. 
- Across the top is a slim stats strip showing the total number of restored spots, total kilograms of waste collected, and points awarded across the neighbourhood.
- You can filter posts by:
  - **All:** Everything posted.
  - **Restored:** Beautiful Before & After successes.
  - **Needs Clean:** Open areas waiting for helpers.
  - **Nearby:** Spots closest to your current location.

### Step 2: Report a Dirty Spot
1. Click the green **"+ Report dirty spot"** button in the header.
2. **Step 1 (Photo):** Snap a clear photo of the litter using your phone camera or upload a file.
3. **Step 2 (Location):** Type the landmark (e.g., *"Near Town Library"* or *"School Playground North Gate"*) or click *"Use GPS"* to locate the spot accurately on the map.
4. **Step 3 (Details):** Give it a short title (e.g., *"Discarded chips packets and plastic wrappers"*), select the severity level (Low, Medium, High), and hit **Submit**.
5. Your spot is instantly pinned on the map with a volunteer bounty!

### Step 3: Clean the Spot & Submit Proof
1. When you or your volunteer group cleans a spot, click **"Clean this spot"** on the card.
2. Snap an **"After" photo** showing the exact same spot now neat, swept, and garbage-free.
3. The app confirms that your location matches where the trash was originally reported.
4. Hit **Submit Clean Proof**!

### Step 4: Interact with the Split Slider
- The moment your cleanup is submitted, the app automatically stitches your "Before" and "After" photos into an **interactive split slider**.
- Drag the circular slider back and forth to reveal the transformation.
- Neighbours can leave supportive comments and tap the heart to cheer your team on.

### Step 5: Check Points & Rewards
- Visit the **Leaderboard** tab to see where you rank among your schoolmates and neighbours.
- Visit the **Rewards** tab to track your progress bar towards the next rank (e.g., from *Civic Scout* to *Eco Guardian*) and redeem volunteer certificates.

## 🤖 5. Possibilities for Artificial Intelligence (AI) in EcoScan

Artificial Intelligence can take EcoScan from a helpful community tool to a **cutting-edge environmental platform**. Here are 6 exciting features AI can add in the future:

### 1. 🔍 AI Smart Trash Identifier (Computer Vision)
- **How it works:** When a student snaps a photo of a garbage heap, an on-device AI model scans the image and automatically tags what kind of litter is present.
- **Example:** The AI automatically detects: *"65% single-use plastic, 20% cardboard, 15% glass bottles."*
- **Why it helps:** Volunteers know in advance whether they need heavy-duty gloves (for glass) or extra sacks (for bulky plastics).

### 2. ♻️ Smart Waste Segregation Guide
- **How it works:** After recognizing the items in the photo, the AI gives volunteers clear instructions on where to dispose of them:
  - *Dry Waste / Recyclables:* Clean plastic bottles, paper cups, soda cans.
  - *Wet / Organic Waste:* Fruit peels, leaves, food scraps (for composting).
  - *Hazardous Waste:* Old batteries, tube lights, electronic scrap.
  - **Why it helps:** Ensures trash doesn't just get shifted from the ground into a mixed dump, but actually gets recycled.

### 3. ✅ AI Before & After Verification (Anti-Cheat / Quality Check)
- **How it works:** AI compares the background buildings, trees, and ground texture between the "Before" photo and the "After" photo.
- **Why it helps:** 
  - Confirms both photos were genuinely taken at the exact same location.
  - Checks if the trash was truly removed or if the photo was taken from an angle that hid the garbage.
  - Prevents fake submissions and guarantees 100% genuine awards.

### 4. ⚖️ AI Waste Weight & Bounty Estimator
- **How it works:** By analyzing the depth, pile size, and density of objects in the photo, the AI predicts: *"Estimated weight: 14 kg of waste."*
- **Why it helps:** Automatically calculates fair bounty points so that larger, more difficult messes award more points to volunteers.

### 5. 🗺️ AI Cleanup Heatmaps & Route Planner
- **How it works:** If multiple dirty spots are reported around the school or neighborhood, AI draws an optimal "Cleanup Trail" or walking route.
- **Example:** *"Saturday Morning Route: Clean 3 adjacent street corners within 800 meters in 45 minutes."*
- **Why it helps:** Organizes school eco-walks and Sunday morning volunteer drives efficiently.

### 6. 💬 "EcoBuddy" AI Assistant
- **How it works:** A friendly chatbot inside the app that answers eco-questions in simple words.
- **Example:** A student asks: *"Can we recycle thermocol?"* and EcoBuddy explains how thermocol is processed and where to drop it off locally.

## 🌟 6. Real-Life School & Community Scenarios

### Scenario A: The Annual School Environment Day
On World Environment Day, a high school divides its students into four houses. Each house uses EcoScan to identify littered spots within a 1-kilometer radius of the campus.
